I ask Claude for a strategy memo. It is on my Kindle Scribe a minute later.

I read it in an armchair and cross out the assumption I do not believe, writing
the replacement in the margin.

Then I ask it to read my handwriting. The strike-through becomes a deletion.
The arrow becomes an insertion, in the right place.

Open source, self-hosted, works with Claude, Codex and ChatGPT desktop:
github.com/starkshtlm/kindle-scribe-mcp

**Alternative opening, if the thread needs a hook rather than a story:**

Handwriting is a better review interface than a chat box, and I think the
reason is that a page has *positions*. A note in the margin points at
something. A reply in a chat window does not.
